\section{Innovation features shown}
Voice input (02:20), AI-assisted classification with confidence (02:40), duplicate detection
while typing (02:40, 06:50), smart assignment with explanations (04:00), predictive breach risk
(06:10), Telegram alerts (03:20), Google Sign-In (01:20), the live service health map (10:30),
chatbot reporting (14:20) and suggested fixes from past incidents (14:00).
